| D-05 | 2026-10-26 | VoiceKiller is integrated as a second voice source (key and user id in the Credential Manager). | D-06 | 2026-10-26 | Multilingual: language is a per-character label and a voice-search filter, not a switch sent to the model. Eleven v4 detects the language from the text, so mixed-language lines (Taglish, Pidgin, code-switching) are written as they are spoken and the voice supplies the accent. No accent is claimed until the owner listens. | Eleven v4 lists 90+ languages; docs do not state a v4 language-code switch. |
| D-07 | 2026-10-26 | Every paid action asks first with a character count. Single takes and samples offer a per-session "do not ask again"; batches and voice design always ask. | Owner rule: no unconfirmed spend. |
| D-08 | 2026-10-26 | Engines are where voices come from: ElevenLabs, VoiceKiller, Kie (67 preset voices), Piper (offline) and the owner's own local server. A voice belongs to one engine, so choosing a voice chooses the engine. Offline engines never show a cost sheet. | Owner asked to choose engines and to have local models. |
| D-09 | 2026-10-26 | Piper is the built-in local engine: Windows build downloaded once from its pinned GitHub release (size and SHA-256 checked), voices from the public catalogue (MD5 checked). A local server is accepted only on 127.0.0.1 or localhost. Piper reads plain text, so tags are stripped. Expressive local models (Chatterbox and similar) are reached through the local-server connector until they can be installed in one click. | Piper is small, CPU-only and covers many languages; Chatterbox needs Python and a GPU. |
| D-10 | 2026-10-26 | The Soundstage PRD-002 is not the plan for Greenroom. Greenroom keeps only: script, voices and engines, directing with chips, takes, export, and later an AI Director and music. Video, hardware profiles, a model registry and a timeline are out of scope unless the owner asks. | Owner asked whether the PRD was overkill; it is what made Soundstage feel heavy. |
| D-11 | 2026-10-26 | ElevenLabs plan limits on saving, adding or cloning voices are explained in plain words with the options that still work (premade voices, VoiceKiller, Kie, Piper). | Owner hit an upgrade prompt when saving a voice. |
